Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

ManyHandz ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our platform at manyhandz.ai.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: Name, email address, and business details you provide when signing up.
  • Usage data: How you interact with the platform, including messages sent to your AI assistant.
  • Business data: Customer records, jobs, invoices, and other data you enter into the platform.
  • Payment information: Processed securely via Stripe. We do not store card details.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the ManyHandz service
  • To process payments and manage your subscription
  • To send service-related communications (account notices, invoices)
  • To respond to support requests

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers hosted by Supabase, with infrastructure in Australia and the US. We use industry-standard encryption for data in transit and at rest.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We only share data with third-party service providers necessary to operate the platform (Supabase, Stripe, Anthropic for AI processing). All providers are bound by data processing agreements.

5. AI Processing

Messages sent to your AI assistant may be processed by Anthropic's Claude API to generate responses. We do not use your data to train AI models. Anthropic's privacy policy applies to this processing.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by emailing hello@manyhandz.ai.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at hello@manyhandz.ai.

8. Cookies

We use essential cookies for authentication and session management only. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at hello@manyhandz.ai or write to ManyHandz, Perth, Western Australia.
